# Five new custom field types, now ready to copy between accounts

*By vyntec Marketing, Advertising, Branding & Development*

The records in your system are only as useful as the fields that hold them. Plain text and dropdowns cover a lot, but some information has a shape of its own: a link, an appointment window, a formatted note, the team member who owns a record. Your vyntec system now supports five custom field types built for exactly that.

## The new field types
- **URL**: store a link, like a listing, a shared folder or a booking page, as a link rather than loose text.
- **Date & Time**: capture a date with a time, not just a day.
- **Rich text**: keep formatted notes (headings, lists, emphasis) on a record.
- **Users**: point a record at a person on your team.
- **Images in Radio and Checkbox fields**: show a picture next to each choice, so options are easier to tell apart.

## What changed this week
Two things make these fields practical for an account build like yours:

1. **They copy with the rest of your setup.** When we build a configuration once and roll it out to another location or account, these field types now come along with it. Nobody has to recreate them by hand, and every account stays consistent.
2. **They work with integrations.** The fields can now be created and filled in programmatically, so the tools you connect to your system can read and write them like any other field.

## What it means for you
If you're a vyntec client, there's nothing to buy and nothing to switch on: improvements like this are part of your existing package. If one of these field types would make a record in your system clearer, tell us which one and where, and we'll add it to your build.
