# SOP: "It's live" email (head-of-customer-relations)

When a `Go live: <what> — <client>` item is Done (QA PASS, Evidence, client approval if needed), head-of-customer-relations writes one Gmail **draft** to the client contact, in the existing thread (search Gmail first). Never sent by an agent: the task goes to Needs your review.

Subject: It's live — <what> | <Client>

Hi <first name>,

<What> is live as of <date>. Here's what that means for you:
- <what changed, in their words, 1–3 bullets>
- <where to see it: link (link.vyntec.co or the live URL)>

What we checked: <one line from Evidence, e.g. "we ran a test booking and the confirmation arrived">.

Anything that looks off, reply here and we'll look at it the same day.

Talk soon,
Vinny Mele · vyntec
Vinny@vyntec.co / 407-969-9070 · vyntec.co

Rules: white label (no vendor name); no prices, metrics or promises that aren't in the task; mark the draft PENDING APPROVAL in the task.
